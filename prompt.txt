## Mandatory merge decision rules

The consolidation targets above are based on the architecture tree, not verified implementation duplication.

For every proposed merge:

1. Inspect implementations, dependencies, consumers and responsibilities.
2. Merge only cohesive functionality with compatible ownership and lifecycle.
3. Preserve independent security, transaction, provider, caching and lazy-loading boundaries.
4. Review Python files exceeding 500 lines and React files exceeding 350 lines. These are review thresholds, not mandatory splitting rules.
5. If a merge creates circular imports, excessive complexity or weaker boundaries, retain the files separately and document the reason.
6. Remove obsolete files only after migrating all imports and verifying behavior.
7. Do not introduce replacement abstractions that negate the simplification.
